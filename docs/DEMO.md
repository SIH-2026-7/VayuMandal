1. Open the landing page. Explain that the project addresses SIH PS 26078 and show the two-stage architecture.
2. Launch the platform. Point out the explicit synthetic-data label and fixed issue date.
3. Select cyclone and change day 3 to day 10. Observe the moving centroid and ensemble interval. Play and pause the forecast.
4. Change to heatwave, then cold wave. EFI changes direction for low-temperature anomalies. Adjust ensemble size.
5. Switch layers and zoom. Click the map to measure distance from the current centroid.
6. Open Downscaling lab. Compare the constructed field with its smoothed version; explain that this is a smoothing demonstration, not trained diffusion evidence.
7. Open Spatial alerts. Mark reviewed, export GeoJSON, and inspect the REST response. Explain that no real notifications are sent.
8. Open Methodology. Discuss data access, training, physical checks, holdouts and calibration as production milestones.
